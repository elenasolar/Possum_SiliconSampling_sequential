Climate scientists are less unanimous about when the world will reach net-zero CO₂ because it depends on future policy, technology adoption, and economic choices, not just physical laws. In a 2024 survey of 211 Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change report authors, 66% believed the world would reach net-zero CO₂ before 2085 (Wynes et al., 2024). Estimates like these are updated as new evidence emerges, such as changes in emissions trends, new policies, and improvements in clean-energy technologies.
Source:
Wynes, S., Davis, S. J., Dickau, M., Ly, S., Maibach, E., Rogelj, J., ... & Matthews, H. D. (2024). Perceptions of carbon dioxide emission reductions and future warming among climate experts. Communications Earth & Environment, 5(1), 498
